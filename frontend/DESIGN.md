## Brand & Style

FounderGate uses a premium, precise, near-black base aesthetic. We emphasize restrained cyan telemetry, strong typographic hierarchy, accessible contrast, purposeful whitespace, and no fake dashboards or decorative controls.

The UI communicates that synthesis remains locked until the server confirms the human gate.

## Colors
Deep, near-black backgrounds with high contrast text. Cyan/Teal (#0e7490, #0891b2) is used for key accents, metrics, and active states. Red (#b91c1c) is used for rejected claims or assumption debt.

## Typography
Clean, legible sans-serif (Inter) focusing on high readability.

## Layout & Spacing
Responsive grid. Split panes for evidence review (e.g. transcript on left, claims on right). Ample whitespace to separate critical decision areas.

## Components
Cards are used to present claims. Buttons have sharp or slightly rounded corners. Badges for status (Accepted, Rejected, Unresolved).
